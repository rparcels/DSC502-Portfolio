Details

This plot is a heatmap of horsepower vs. 0–60 times. Unlike a scatter plot, which shows individual vehicles, a heatmap emphasizes where the data is most concentrated. By binning the horsepower and acceleration values and coloring each bin by density, the plot highlights regions where many vehicles share similar performance stats. The heatmap provides a more aggregated view of the original data and helps reveal broader patterns that can be harder to notice when looking at individual points.
